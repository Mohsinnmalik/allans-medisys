Powerful Imaging

Samsung V7

Designed as a versatile diagnostic platform delivering powerful imaging with consistent results and a compact, mobile design suited to busy practices.

Intelligent
Clinical Workflow

The V7 is a general-purpose diagnostic ultrasound system combining advanced beamforming, sophisticated image processing, and state-of-the-art transducers to provide image clarity and penetration.


It combines imaging technologies, Doppler tools, and AI-assisted clinical features in a compact mobile system for general imaging, women's health, and point-of-care.

TISSUE STIFFNESS MAPPING

S-Shearwave Imaging™

Provides real-time tissue-stiffness mapping using colour-coded visualization. Supporting reproducible measurements for applications such as liver fibrosis and breast lesions, with a Reliability Measurement Index to assess measurement quality.

AI-BASED PROCEDURES

NerveTrack™

NerveTrack™ uses AI-based technology to highlight nerves in real time, supporting clinicians during procedures where accurate nerve identification is important, such as nerve blocks and other point-of-care applications.

Advanced Innovations

HQ-Vision™

Designed to reduce natural blurring in ultrasound imaging, resulting in a sharper and more defined view of anatomical structures at different depths.

SonoSync™

Enables real-time sharing of ultrasound examinations for collaboration, communication, care guidance and training between specialists and sonographers.

BatteryAssist™

Provides battery power when AC power is temporarily unavailable, allowing the system to be moved to another location without shutting down and restarting.
