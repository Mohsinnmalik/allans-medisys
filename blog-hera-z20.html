Flagship Women's Health Innovation

Samsung HERA Z20

Empowering Women With Vision

Inspired by the Greek concept of protection and hospitality ("Zena"), the HERA Z20 redefines maternal-fetal and gynecological ultrasound with 2nd Generation Crystal Architecture™, Deep Learning clinical AI, and an immersive 27" OLED monitor.

Crystal Architecture™
2nd Generation

The HERA Z20 integrates the pinnacle of Samsung ultrasound engineering, uniting CrystalBeam™ advanced beamforming, CrystalLive™ multi-dimensional processing, and cutting-edge S-Vue™ Transducers.


This synergistic platform delivers unprecedented image clarity, deeper penetration, superior contrast resolution, and crisp tissue boundaries to resolve even the most challenging obstetrical examinations with confidence.

Intelligent Diagnostic Accuracy

Deep Learning Clinical AI

Live ViewAssist™ automatically classifies ultrasound images in real time while providing automatic anatomical annotations and measurement results. Paired with BiometryAssist™, clinicians can measure fetal growth parameters with a single click, drastically reducing scan time and exam inconsistency.

Advanced Hemodynamics

MV-Flow™ & LumiFlow™

Visualize microcirculatory and slow blood flow with extraordinary precision. LumiFlow™ provides a 3D-like intuitive visualization of blood flow, enabling physicians to understand the vascular structure of small vessels, placental villous trees, and fetal cardiac anatomy with clarity.

Volume Rendering Excellence

RealisticVue™ & CrystalVue™

RealisticVue™ renders high-resolution 3D anatomy with realistic depth perception and adjustable light direction. CrystalVue™ enhances both internal and external structural boundaries in a single view, while PortraitVue™ AI technology restores facial features for an extraordinary bonding experience.

Every Stage of Women's Health

Tailored diagnostic solutions across fertility care, fetal diagnosis, labor & delivery, and gynecology.

Fetal Heart Diagnosis

HeartAssist™ automatically classifies ultrasound images into required fetal heart diagnostic measurement views, delivering immediate standardized cardiac reports.

Uterine Health Assessment

UterineAssist™ and UterineContour™ extract curved endometrium centerlines in 3D coronal view, reporting malformations according to ESHRE/ESGE and ASRM guidelines.

27" OLED & 15.6" Touchscreen

State-of-the-art 27-inch OLED monitor provides true deep blacks and vivid color rendition, accompanied by a flexible motorized console and low-noise cooling architecture.

SonoSync™ Collaboration

Live-stream real-time ultrasound examinations with remote control capabilities on PC, tablet, or smartphone for training, care guidance, and remote specialist consultation.

CMV1-10 Matrix Transducer

Cutting-edge matrix array transducer technology delivering uniform elevation resolution and penetration across 2D, 3D, and color flow examinations.

Eco-Conscious Design

Crafted with 50% recycled resin system body and 100% eco-friendly shockproof paper packaging, uniting healthcare innovation with environmental sustainability.
