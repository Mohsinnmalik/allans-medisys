Advanced Imaging & Workflow

Samsung HS50

Modern diagnostic ultrasound requires a balance of image quality, clinical versatility and efficient workflow. The Samsung HS50 is an advanced general-purpose platform designed to support a broad range of clinical imaging requirements.

Powered by
CrystalLive™

At the centre of the HS50 is Samsung's CrystalLive™ imaging engine with enhanced 2D image processing and colour signal processing designed to provide strong image performance and efficient workflow during complex cases.


Additionally, HQ-Vision™ is designed to provide clearer ultrasound images by addressing characteristics that can make structures appear less sharp than actual vision, ensuring unparalleled clarity.

ADVANCED IMAGING

ClearVision

ClearVision uses noise-reduction and edge-enhancement processing to create sharper 2D images. Samsung also describes application-specific optimization and improved temporal resolution during live scanning.

IMAGE CLARITY

S-Harmonic™

S-Harmonic™ uses pulse-inversion technology to improve image clarity and reduce signal noise, supporting more uniform imaging from near to far field for highly accurate clinical assessments.

Smart Assistant Tools

S-Flow™

A directional Power Doppler imaging technology designed to help visualize peripheral blood vessels. This can be highly valuable when blood-flow assessment is part of the examination.

LumiFlow™

Presents blood-flow information in a three-dimensional-like visualization to help users understand the structure and distribution of blood flow and small vessels more intuitively.

ElastoScan™

An elasticity-imaging technology that can display relative tissue elasticity in colour within a selected region of interest, aiding in accurate clinical diagnosis.
