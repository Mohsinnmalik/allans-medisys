Women's Health Redefined

Samsung HS40

Experience the perfect harmony of CrystalLive™ AI-assisted imaging and ergonomic design, tailored specifically for routine Obstetrics & Gynecology.

Advanced Imaging
Meets Smart Design

At the core of the HS40 is Samsung’s cutting-edge image processing architecture. Engineered to mitigate noise, enhance boundaries, and provide crystal-clear fetal and anatomical visualization, the HS40 ensures diagnostic confidence in every exam.


CrystalLive™ provides enhanced 2D image processing, 3D rendering, and color signal processing for clearer, more natural-looking images.

ENHANCED CLARITY

ClearVision™

A sophisticated noise reduction filter that improves tissue interface definition and creates sharper 2D images. It significantly reduces artifacts and enhances boundaries, providing unprecedented clarity in dense tissues.

FETAL RENDERING

RealisticVue™ 3D

Supports advanced 4D functions providing incredibly detailed, life-like 3D/4D images. This high-resolution rendering is essential for detailed diagnostic assessment and beautiful fetal-maternal bonding moments.

Intelligent 5D Solutions

5D NT™

Provides an intuitive, semi-automated measurement of the fetal nuchal translucency, improving consistency and reducing dependency on user skill.

BiometryAssist™

A semi-automatic tool that simplifies fetal biometry measurements (HC, BPD, AC, FL) with a single click, automating routine workflow.

5D Follicle™

Identifies and measures multiple ovarian follicles in a single 3D dataset, providing a rapid assessment during IVF monitoring.
