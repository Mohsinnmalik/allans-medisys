Next-Generation Surgical Imaging

Flat Panel C-Arm

ALLANS MENTOR-FP (3.5 / 5 kW)

Experience the breakthrough in intraoperative fluoroscopy. The ALLANS MENTOR-FP combines a high-resolution Flat Panel Detector (FPD) with 40 kHz High-Frequency generator technology, delivering crystal-clear, distortion-free images at significantly lower radiation doses.

The Flat Panel
Advantage (FPD)

Traditional image intensifiers (I.I.) suffer from image distortion, optical vignetting, and degradation over time. The ALLANS MENTOR-FP utilizes modern Flat Panel Detector technology to eliminate these limitations entirely.


FPD offers superior image resolution, edge-to-edge sharpness, a broader field of view in a single exposure, and ultra-low radiation doses, ensuring safer surgeries for both patients and medical teams.

Centralized Control

Intuitive Digital Touch Console

The ALLANS Medical C-Arm Console Board is engineered as a user-friendly, central interface empowering surgeons and technicians with comprehensive control. Features instant Auto Mode, independent kV (up to 110 kV) and mA/mAs adjustments, laser collimator controls, and clear digital diagnostic displays.

Uncompromised Surgical Agility

5-Axis Precision Movements

Engineered for complex orthopedic, vascular, and general surgical procedures. Features 127° orbital rotation, ±180° rotation in the vertical plane, ±12° wig-wag swivel, 20 cm horizontal carriage movement, and 42 cm motorized vertical travel with counter-balanced steering and instant locking.

Engineered for Operating Room Excellence

High-frequency performance, robust DICOM workflow, and certified radiation safety.

40 kHz High-Frequency Power

High Voltage Generator operating at 40 kHz (3.5 kW / 5 kW), delivering up to 110 kV and 80 mA with minimal production of harmful soft radiation.

Dual Screen Medical Monitors

Dedicated high-luminance dual displays for real-time fluoroscopy and reference image comparison with high contrast ratio and anti-reflective coating.

DICOM 3.0 & PACS Integration

Comprehensive digital imaging software with DICOM Print, Store, Worklist, and USB export for seamless connectivity to hospital HIS/PACS networks.

Advanced Image Processing

Recursive Noise Reduction, Digital Zoom & PAN, Image Rotation, Horizontal/Vertical Flip, Magnifying Glass, and Angle/Distance annotation tools.

Certified Safety Standards

Full regulatory compliance and certified safety standards: BIS:7620, AERB, ISO 13485, CE Mark, and CDSCO approved for clinical peace of mind.

Single-Phase Standard Power

Operates effortlessly on standard single-phase 230V, 16A, 50Hz electrical supply with maximum current absorption of only 6A.
